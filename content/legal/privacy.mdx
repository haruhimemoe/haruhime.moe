## Each tool has its own policy

The tools run on their own subdomains and keep what you make there (packs, pools, saved BBCode, tournaments) in their own databases. Each has its own privacy policy, which applies there instead of this one:

- [packs privacy policy](https://packs.haruhime.moe/legal/privacy)
- [pools privacy policy](https://pools.haruhime.moe/legal/privacy)
- [bb privacy policy](https://bb.haruhime.moe/legal/privacy)
- [harumin privacy policy](https://harumin.haruhime.moe/legal/privacy)
- [tourney privacy policy](https://tourney.haruhime.moe/legal/privacy)
